# Open issues

- **`CalibrationMasters::build` takes five parameters.** `frames`, `sigma_threshold`, `ingest`, `cache` and `monitor`, a public entry point called from `lens` and the lumos examples, tests and benches.
- **lumos cosmic-ray rejection builds its detector anew for every frame.** `cosmic_ray::reject` builds a fresh `BayerDetector` or `MonoDetector` on each call from `MosaicPreparation::prepare`, so every light allocates the detector's five `f32` scratch planes and three masks again; the pipeline's `DetectorPool` keeps a `StarDetector` per frame in flight, and nothing keeps a cosmic-ray detector.
